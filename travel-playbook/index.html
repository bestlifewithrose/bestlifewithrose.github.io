How I stretch my travel budget with points.

I'm not a points expert. I'm a nurse who learned by doing, and got a few things wrong along the way. Here's what I wish someone had told me at the start.

New to points? Read Where I started, then open the glossary before anything else.
You know the basics. Compare final checkout totals and keep your points flexible until plans are certain.
Start with the transfer rule, annual credits, and the most expensive hotel nights.

Where I started

+
Keep points flexible until plans are certain.

When uncertainty exists, like waiting on a visa, flexibility beats max value. For my Italy flight, I booked through Chase Travel for 83,518 points + $0 cash instead of transferring to United.

“It provides more security cause I can rebook.”

What I got wrong

+
Never transfer a full balance speculatively.

Find the exact award flight first, confirm the mileage price, then transfer only the number of points needed.

Compare final checkout totals, not teaser prices.

One booking site showed $372 but $465 at checkout. The portal showed $571.33 minus my $100 credit, which came to $471.33. That was only $6.33 more, so I kept my points.

What I'd do again

+
Annual credits first.

Use-it-or-lose-it benefits get spent before points are burned. I use my $100 annual hotel credit by booking through the travel portal to trigger it, then I apply points to the rest.

Use free night awards on the most expensive nights.

I target the priciest stays for award redemptions.

Beginner glossary

Points

Rewards earned in a credit card program. Some can be used in a travel portal or transferred to partners.

Miles

Rewards held inside an airline loyalty program and used under that program's rules.

Transfer partners

Airline or hotel programs that can receive points from a credit card rewards program.

Annual fee

The amount a card charges each year for keeping the account open.

Statement credit

A credit that reduces the amount shown on your card statement when its terms are met.

My pause-before-you-click list
